FAQ

Common Questions

Do you post reviews without approval?

No. I prepare review responses as drafts and publish only after you authorize them.

Which time zone do you work in?

I am based in Dhaka, Bangladesh and work during US business hours so we can talk when it suits your team.

How do you handle patient privacy?

I never ask for patient medical information. Review replies stay general and never confirm that someone was a patient or mention treatment details.

Do I need to give you account passwords?

No. Access is requested through official manager or admin invitations, which you can remove at any time.

How do we get started?

Send a message or book a short call. I will review your Google profile and social pages and suggest next steps.
